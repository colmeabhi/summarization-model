How it works

This is an extractive summariser. It does not write new sentences, it picks the most useful sentences from your text and keeps them in the same order.

The pipeline

  1. 1 Split the text into sentences
  2. 2 Remove stopwords and stem the words
  3. 3 Score every sentence with 8 features
  4. 4 Smooth the scores with PCA (in place of an RBM)
  5. 5 Pick the best sentences and put them back in order

The 8 features

FeatureWhat it measures
Thematic wordsHow many of the 10 most common words in the text the sentence has
Sentence positionWhere the sentence is in the text (start and end are usually important)
Sentence lengthVery short sentences (under 3 words) get 0, the rest are compared to the longest one
Proper nounsHow many names of people, places, etc. the sentence has
NumbersSentences with numbers usually have facts in them
Named entitiesNumber of named entities NLTK finds in the sentence
TF-ISFLike TF-IDF but over sentences instead of documents
Centroid similarityCosine similarity to the sentence with the highest TF-ISF

Example

Input (5 sentences): Highways are lines of communication. They connect cities and towns. In 2020, over 5 million people used the National Highway every day. Roads also help trade grow. Experts say this will boost the economy.

Short summary: Highways are lines of communication. Experts say this will boost the economy.

Using the API

You can also call it from code:

curl -X POST http://localhost:5001/api/summarize \
     -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
     -d '{"text": "Your text here.", "length": "short"}'
